Inclusion Criteria
1 The study pertains to the field of SE or addresses SD (activity, tasks, job role). 
2 The study identifies more than one individual competency or describes a kind of competency model (framework, profile) or describes a curriculum.
3 The study proposes a competency model.
4 The model captures a collection of soft competencies.
5 The study answers the research questions
6 The paper must be peer-reviewed
7 The paper must be written in English

Exclusion Criteria
1 The study concerns teaching a single course or subject matter.
2 The study focuses on organizational competencies.
3 The study does not propose a competency model, proposes a model other than a competency model, or proposes a model that is not a result of the study.
4 The model includes fewer than five soft competencies.
